Platform overview

Three tools that turn a story into a funded company.

01

Pitch deck builder

A drag-and-drop pitch deck editor with investor-grade templates covering pre-seed narrative, Series A traction decks, public company investor day presentations, and credit facility information memoranda. Live data connectors pull financials directly from accounting software, updating revenue charts, margin graphs, and KPI tables automatically each time a new deck version is generated for a different investor meeting.

02

Comparable company analysis

An automated comps table generator that selects the ten most relevant publicly traded peers based on sector, geography, revenue stage, and business model, and presents EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E, and price-to-sales multiples in a formatted table suitable for inclusion in a pitch or management information circular. Multiples update daily from exchange data, eliminating the manual data gathering that occupies investment banking analysts on every deal.

03

Investor data room

A secure virtual data room with granular permission controls allowing founders to share specific deck slides, financial models, and due diligence documents with individual investors without creating a public link. Engagement analytics show which slides investors spent the most time on, how many times each page was viewed, and whether documents were shared with colleagues, giving founders intelligence to calibrate follow-up conversations.
